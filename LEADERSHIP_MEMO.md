2. **Audit North's loss-making orders — Regional Commercial and Finance.** Nine negative-contribution delivered orders in 2025 total INR 8,424.50 in absolute losses, 2.28% of company net revenue. Review the order-level refund, discount, product-cost, and shipping components before changing pricing or eligibility rules. **Success KPI:** reduce negative-contribution order share and absolute loss exposure in comparable future purchase cohorts, with total contribution as a guardrail. **Limitation:** these losses overlap category refunds, including the first action; the exposures must not be added. North's category-mix-standardized margin is strongest, so its raw regional margin does not justify a blanket regional cutback.

Investigate return concentration, discount mix, and unit costs, then pilot a targeted intervention. **Success KPI:** mature unit-return rate and weighted category contribution margin, with paid sales and customer experience as guardrails. **Limitation:** growing sales with weaker margins is descriptive, and no category meets the formal growth-plus-five-point-deterioration rule.
